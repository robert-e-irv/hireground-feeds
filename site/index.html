How it works

Add your resume and where you live. Jobs you can actually take are ranked by how well they match and how recently they were posted.

Your resume is saved in this browser only. Only the job titles and the place you search for go to the job boards.

Matching is by place name, not distance. Remote jobs are kept only if they're open to your country or region.

Your resume, saved jobs and settings stay in this browser. Nothing goes to the job boards.
